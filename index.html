Hi, I'm Alex

Sr Manager Data & Analytics, Professional Runner

I work as a Senior Manager of Data & Analytics at United Way Boston, where I collaborate with colleagues to turn complex data into clear, actionable insights. My academic background at Northeastern University - earning a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Communications and a graduate certificate in Information Design and Data Visualization - gave me a strong foundation in Python, SQL, Power BI, and a wide range of tools and frameworks. Over the years, I’ve advanced from co‑op roles to senior leadership, contributing to projects that streamlined financial reporting, automated survey feedback pipelines, and redesigned dashboards to improve efficiency.

Throughout my journey, I’ve valued being both a teammate and a leader. As captain of Northeastern’s Division I track & field and cross country teams, I learned how to motivate and support others while striving for excellence, ultimately becoming an NCAA All‑American and Olympic Trials qualifier before signing with Saucony to run professionally. I bring that same balance of collaboration and leadership into my current role, mentoring interns, guiding analytics initiatives, and working alongside colleagues to uncover meaningful stories in data and present them in ways that inspire action and drive results.
